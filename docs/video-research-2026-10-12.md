# Public video research — October 12, 2026

This pass shifts the site from broad system prose toward directly evidenced reference data. Guides remain exactly three. Public sources were searched across developer announcements, YouTube, firsthand previews, community discussions and fan databases. Search snippets and article titles were treated as leads, not as sufficient evidence of a stat or unlock.

## Method

Enumerated the public Funselektor video and Shorts listings, retrieved available English captions, and inspected video frames around relevant menus and demonstrations. Reviewed the IGN Demo gameplay plus OverTake and Camodo gameplay, with targeted menu-frame inspection rather than claiming to have watched every internet video in full. Public video files and extracted frames were used temporarily for research; they are not copied into the website or distributed as assets. The published source registry records video title, channel, publication date, duration and checking date. Individual facts link to a playback timestamp.

Automatic captions were useful for finding spoken explanations, but menu values and important claims were checked against visible frames. Videos that only showed driving silhouettes were not used to identify names or infer specifications. A video publication date is not a build number.

Chinese Bilibili results were also searched. The Triple-i showcase's Algeria description was discoverable, but the direct video page returned HTTP 412. Its equivalent official YouTube trailer was reviewed for the published terrain observations instead; this report does not claim a full playback review of inaccessible Bilibili videos.
