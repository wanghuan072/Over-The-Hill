The Roamer brought more room and a boxier idea of adventure. Its brochure proudly called it aerodynamic, despite a drag coefficient of 0.43, this was technically true in the same way that a brick is more aerodynamic if you round the corners. It later became an unlikely movie star, carrying tourists through a slightly mismanaged dinosaur park.
