The Cumbre

Wagon

Wagon

A mysterious surplus of glass in America gave The Cumbre 21% more of it than its predecessor, plus large windows and a radio antenna hidden in the windshield. Drivers could see more of the mountains while listening to the weather report telling them not to go there, assuming the antenna picked it up. An auxiliary fuel tank let them get lost considerably farther from home.
