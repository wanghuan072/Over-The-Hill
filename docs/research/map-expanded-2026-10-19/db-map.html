Emerald Lake — interactive map

Explore animal spawns, crates, meteorites and starting points across all five release areas. Select an area, filter the markers, then zoom or measure distances.

All five areas were reimported from Steam build 25992246 on October 17. The map now includes 55 changed meteorite spawn positions, with 212 spawn points in total. These are possible spawn positions; a meteorite may not be present at every pin.
