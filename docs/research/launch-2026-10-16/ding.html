The Ding

Japan · AsiaTrailMedium tires · Crawler

Free

The Ding was a product of the great ruler shortage of 1960, with new presses stamping straight body panels quickly enough to send them just about everywhere. From transporting tofu in Japan to firewood in the Alps, an icon was created.
